# Explanation audit: all 460 catalogue questions

Audit date: 2026-08-17

## Result

All 460 German and English explanations currently covered by this file were checked against:

1. the exact BAMF question shown in the app;
2. the expected answer in the official BAMF catalogue/online test;
3. the relevant primary law or authoritative public source; and
4. the paired translation for consistency and unsupported claims.

After the corrections recorded below, no known factual contradiction, wrong answer explanation, or German/English mismatch remains in ALL-001 through ALL-300 or any of the 16 state sets.

This audit distinguishes stable facts from facts that can change. It does not treat a current office-holder, parliamentary composition, statistic, or election rule as permanently valid.

## Facts requiring re-checking

These explanations were correct on the audit date but should be re-checked before a catalogue release and at least quarterly:

- ALL-023: employment composition/statistical claim.
- ALL-066: relative size of Jewish communities.
- ALL-072: current Federal Chancellor.
- ALL-073: two largest Bundestag parliamentary groups and seat count.
- ALL-075: current Federal President and term end.
- ALL-117, ALL-121, ALL-123 and ALL-127: electoral threshold/rules if federal election law changes.
- ALL-213: Germany's population; the catalogue's 84 million is a rounded figure (83.5 million at the end of 2025).
- ALL-221: temporary internal Schengen border controls.
- ALL-230: latest European election date.
- ALL-236: number of EU member states.
- ALL-256: restaurant licensing because state laws and the type of service can change the requirement.
- ALL-266: quiet hours because the precise rule is local and contractual rather than uniformly national.
- ALL-268: languages offered for the driving-licence theory test.
- ALL-269: statutory childcare entitlement.
- ALL-297: largest migration-origin group because the result depends on statistical definition and date.
- State questions 03, 04, 06, 09 and 10: parliamentary terms, voting ages, civic-education institutions, government titles and ministry structures if state law or organisation changes.

Historical “Ampelkoalition” in ALL-103 is a definition of a colour coalition, not a claim that this coalition currently governs Germany.

## Verification policy for later edits

An explanation is not considered audited merely because its expected option is correct. Any edit must still be checked for scope, legal nuance, date sensitivity, German/English equivalence, and compatibility with shuffled answer options. Update the audit date and correction table whenever audited text changes.
